# Asset provenance

## Generated images

Created with the built-in image generation tool for this project on October 15–16, 2026. No third-party product photography was downloaded.

- `public/images/hero.webp`: original cognac-handbag campaign still life, converted from generated PNG to WebP.
- `public/images/aurora.webp`, `heritage.webp`, `noir.webp`, `loafer.webp`, `derby.webp`, `atelier.webp`: six product panels extracted from a generated contact sheet.
- `public/images/women.webp`, `men.webp`, `craft.webp`: editorial panels extracted from the same generated contact sheet.

The contact sheet and hero are separate conceptual renders; fine product details may differ. Final commercial imagery should be replaced with approved photographs of actual products. No model identity or existing fashion-house branding was requested.

- `public/images/floating-bag.webp`, `floating-shoe.webp`: isolated transparent product renders generated for the revised scroll choreography, extracted and optimized from a two-object asset sheet.
